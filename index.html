How to Log Your Walk on Strava
Step 01
📱
Open Strava
Launch the Strava app before you start walking. Don't begin without pressing Record.
Step 02
▶️
Press Record
Tap Record → select Walk. Your session begins now.
Step 03
🚶
Walk!
Complete your walk in one continuous go. Keep the app running throughout.
Step 04
⏹️
Press Finish
Tap Finish and save the activity. That single session is your official log.
⚠️
One attempt per day — no exceptions. Only a single continuous Strava activity will be accepted each day. If multiple walks are logged, HR will count only the first recorded session. Separate activities will not be merged or combined.
How the Leaderboard Works

Your total step count is 3,500 + every extra step you walk above the minimum. The more you walk, the higher your total — and the higher you rank on the leaderboard.

The leaderboard is updated every Monday by HR, reflecting the previous week's totals. Step counts accumulate across all weeks.

Rules & FAQs
Q Does the step target stay the same every week?
No — the step target changes every week! Week 1: 3.5K, Week 2: 5K, Week 3: 7.5K, Week 4: 10K, Week 5: 12.5K. HR will also announce via the Teams channel and email.
Q What do I get if I win the challenge?
The top performers on the leaderboard walk away with an exciting surprise gift 🎁 — but honestly, the biggest win is the one you can't wrap in a box. Better energy, a healthier body, and a habit that stays with you long after the challenge ends. Your health is the real prize. 💚
Q Can I log multiple walks in a day?
No. Only one Strava activity per day is accepted. You must start and finish in a single session. Multiple separate logs will not be combined — only the first recorded activity counts.
Q What if I forget to press Record before walking?
Walks not tracked live on Strava cannot be counted. Manual entries or retroactive edits are not accepted. Always press Record before you begin.
Q Can I pause mid-walk?
Yes — pausing and resuming the same Strava session is fine (e.g. for traffic or a water break). Just don't end the activity and start a new one.
Q What if I miss the 3.5K minimum in a week?
Whatever steps you managed that week will still be recorded and added to your total. You simply won't have crossed the 3,500 minimum — but your steps are never lost. Keep going and build on them the next week.
Q Is my location or route data shared with HR?
No. HR only sees your total step count. Your route, map, and location history remain entirely private to you.
Q What if Strava crashes mid-walk?
If Strava auto-saves an incomplete activity, contact HR on the Teams channel with a screenshot. Such cases are reviewed individually and HR's decision is final.
Q Can I walk during lunch or after work hours?
Absolutely! You can walk any time of day — morning, lunch break, or evening. The challenge is flexible; just make sure Strava is running when you do.
Q Can I walk inside the office premises?
No. Walking within office premises is not permitted for this challenge. All walks must take place outside — parks, pavements, neighbourhoods, or any outdoor area. Only outdoor Strava activities will be accepted.
